FAQ

Common questions about research-use sourcing, warehouse options, documentation, account access, and kits.

Are products for research use only?

Yes. Products are intended for laboratory research use only. They are not for human consumption, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or disease prevention.

What is US Warehouse fulfillment?

US Warehouse is intended for faster fulfillment and domestic availability. Current catalog offerings are listed from US Warehouse.

What is the typical US Warehouse delivery timeline?

Current site copy uses 2–5 day delivery as the planning estimate. Timelines are not guaranteed and can vary by product, destination, documentation needs, and fulfillment conditions.

Do products include COAs or third-party testing?

Documentation/testing may be available on select products. Availability can vary by product, batch, and sourcing path. Optional upgraded testing may affect fulfillment or shipping timelines.

Why do I need to register before accessing Shop Peptides?

Registration supports responsible product access, researcher verification, account history, shipment tracking, and a cleaner support workflow. Shop access remains locked until registration and email verification are complete.

How does email verification unlock the shop?

Create an account with your email address. We send you a verification message — open it and click the link. Then come back here and log in. Shop Peptides unlocks after your email is confirmed.

Can I order research kits?

Yes. The site includes research kit options and a Build Your Kit inquiry flow. Kit requests are for research-use sourcing inquiries only and should be verified for availability before launch.

Can I order in bulk?

Bulk planning may be supported depending on product and warehouse availability. Message @PepSuppliers on Telegram with product names, vial sizes, and target quantity.

Can Pep Suppliers provide dosing guidance?

No. Pep Suppliers cannot provide medical advice, dosing guidance, human-use guidance, treatment recommendations, or disease-related claims.

How should I contact Pep Suppliers?

Message us on Telegram: @PepSuppliers.
